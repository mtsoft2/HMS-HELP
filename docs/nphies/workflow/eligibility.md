# Eligibility

An eligibility check asks the insurer one question: **is this patient covered
right now, and for what?** The answer comes back in a few seconds.

## When to use it

- When a patient arrives, before you book or treat them.
- Any time you're unsure whether a cover is still active.

## What you do

1. Look up the patient.
2. Run the eligibility check.
3. Read the reply.

## What the reply tells you

- Whether the cover is **active**.
- The **plan** and **network** the patient belongs to.
- The **member details** the insurer holds.
- Any **benefits or limits** the insurer returns (covered categories, amounts
  used and remaining, copays).

## If the patient is not eligible

You'll know straight away, before any service is given. Check the patient's
insurance details are entered correctly and try again; if it's still not active,
the patient's cover may have lapsed.

!!! tip "Manual look-up"
    You can also run an eligibility check by entering a national or Iqama number
    directly, without a full admission — useful at reception for a quick check.
