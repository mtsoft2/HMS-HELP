# Claims

A claim is the **bill for what you did**. You send it after the service, and the
insurer reviews it line by line and tells you how much it will pay.

## Building a claim

The easiest way is from an approved pre-authorization:

1. On the record's **Communication** screen, use **Create Bill** to build the
   patient bill from the approval — items priced, with the patient and insurance
   shares.
2. Use **Create Claim** to turn it into a claim. It copies the patient, visit,
   diagnosis, and only the **approved** items at their approved quantities, and
   attaches the approval reference. Refused lines are left out.

You can also send a claim directly for services that didn't need a prior
approval.

## Sending and reading the result

Send the claim. The insurer adjudicates it and replies. The claim then shows one
of:

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | The insurer accepted the whole claim. |
| **Partially Approved** | Some lines paid, others reduced or refused. |
| **Not Approved** | The claim was refused. |

Each item shows its **approved quantity and amount** next to what you asked for,
the **patient's share**, and the **reason** for any reduction.

## Claim types

The module builds the right shape for each kind of claim automatically:
pharmacy, professional (clinic visit), institutional (inpatient), dental,
optical, and newborn.

!!! tip "Approved Amount"
    On the dashboard and in the lists, **Approved Amount** is the amount the
    insurer agreed to pay (the benefit) — which can be lower than what you
    requested. The per-line detail shows exactly where any difference is.
