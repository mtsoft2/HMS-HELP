# The Overview dashboard

The **Overview** is the manager's view of your NPHIES activity — your numbers
and charts on one page, for whatever date range you choose.

## The date range

A filter at the top sets the period the whole page reports on — today, the last
7, 30, or 90 days, this month, last month, this year, or all time. Your choice
is **remembered** for next time, even on another computer.

## The cards

| Card | Tells you |
|---|---|
| **Claims** | How many claims in the period |
| **Approved** | How many were approved |
| **Rejected** | How many were refused |
| **Pending** | How many are still in progress |
| **Requested Amount** | The total you billed |
| **Approved Amount** | The total the insurer agreed to pay |

## The charts

- A breakdown of claim results (approved / partial / rejected / pending).
- **Requested versus Approved** amount, side by side.
- **Requested versus Approved** quantity.
- A **daily trend** showing the amounts day by day, with the figures on the
  bars.

## The lists

Below the cards, the **Pre-authorizations** and **Claims** lists show every
request with its status, the approved-versus-requested amount and quantity, and
**totals** at the bottom. Open any row to see its items, and use **Validate**,
**Inspect**, or **Send** on the row.

!!! tip
    Use the date filter to compare a slow week against a busy one, or to pull the
    month's approval rate for a report.
